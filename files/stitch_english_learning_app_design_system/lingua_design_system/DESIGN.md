## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for Persian speakers acquiring English, balancing structural rigor with welcoming clarity. The personality rejects superficial, juvenile gamification in favor of deliberate, dignified educational pacing. It communicates quiet confidence, academic integrity, and effortless usability.

The aesthetic fuses **Minimalism** with modern **Corporate / Educational** structure:
- **Calm, High-Focus Surfaces:** Learning demands uninterrupted cognitive bandwidth. Backgrounds are stark, pristine, and light-filled, while typography maintains commanding contrast.
- **Bi-Directional Balance:** The interface natively honors Right-to-Left (RTL) Persian navigational architecture while framing Left-to-Right (LTR) English learning targets inside dedicated cognitive zones.
- **Subtle Modernism:** Tactile feedback is communicated through crisp 1px structural hairbars and clear color-fill states rather than heavy drop shadows, reinforcing a modern digital paper feel.

## Colors

The palette establishes an authoritative educational atmosphere grounded in Royal Azure (`#2169EF`) and supported by sky and deep midnight accents.

### Application Strategy
- **Primary Interactive (`primary-600` / `#2169EF`):** Primary action triggers, progress indicators, active navigation tabs, and prominent interactive cards. In dark mode, this systematically remaps to `primary-400` (`#60A5FA`) for WCAG contrast compliance against deep charcoal surfaces.
- **Tonal Tiers (`primary-100` / `#E8F2FF`):** Passive interactive surfaces, CEFR level badges, active choice selections, and secondary button fills.
- **Semantic Feedback Pairs:**
  - **Success (`#22C55E` / `#DCFCE7`):** Instant validation on correct drills, mastered milestones, and completed units. Paired with `#15803D` for icon and text contrast.
  - **Error (`#EF4444` / `#FEE2E2`):** Incorrect answer selection and form errors, paired with `#B91C1C` for legible corrective feedback.
  - **Warning (`#F59E0B` / `#FEF3C7`):** Review-due items, streak expiration warnings, paired with `#B45309`.
  - **Info (`#0EA5E9` / `#E0F2FE`):** Grammatical tips, phonetic explanations, and contextual aids, paired with `#0369A1`.
- **Gradients:** Reserved solely for hero surfaces, onboarding screens, and major milestone cards: linear progression from `#2169EF` to `#60A5FA` at a 135-degree angle.

## Typography

This system features a dual-engine typography stack engineered for bi-directional language pedagogy.

### Font Implementation Guidelines
- **English UI and Learning Content:** Rendered with `Inter`. Clean, open counters and distinct glyph geometry ensure clarity for non-native learners.
- **Persian UI & Translation Strings:** Rendered with `Vazirmatn`. When rendering Farsi text, apply `Vazirmatn` while preserving the token sizes and line-height multipliers defined in the type scale.
- **Learning Target (`learning-target`):** Specifically designated for English words, phrases, and audio prompts being studied. It runs at `24px` (`20px` on small devices) with relaxed `36px` line-height to make diacritics, stress marks, and punctuation instantly readable.
- **Translation Block (`persian-translation`):** Rendered in RTL orientation with a dedicated line height of `28px` to prevent clipping of extended Persian ascenders and descenders. Colored in `neutral-600` (`#475569`) to ensure clear visual hierarchy beneath primary English terms.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout geometry is anchored to an **8dp rhythmic grid** with a single 4dp half-step (`space-xs`) for fine icon offsets and badge padding.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Mobile Grid (up to 599px):** 4-column fluid layout with `16px` (`margin`) outer boundaries and `16px` (`gutter`) column spacing. Single-task screen paradigm: one drill, question, or vocabulary interaction per screen.
- **Tablet / Large Screen (600px - 1023px):** 8-column split layout with `32px` margins and `24px` gutters. Implements a dual-pane study flow: active exercise panel on the primary side, pedagogical rules, phonetic guide, and vocabulary breakdown on the adjacent side.
- **Desktop / Web Portal (1024px+):** Max layout container capped at `1120px` centered on canvas, 12-column structure with `24px` gutters.

### Component Spacing Rules
- **Interactive Targets:** Every tap target (pronunciation triggers, choices, text inputs, nav elements) maintains a minimum height of `48px`.
- **Card Internals:** Strict `16px` padding around standard learning cards; expanded `24px` internal padding on hero discovery tiles.
- **Badge Bounds:** `8px` vertical, `12px` horizontal padding for CEFR badges and status chips.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy, blurred dropshadows. Depth is articulated purely through tonal stratification, surface border contrasts, and intentional spatial isolation.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Canvas Base):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) in light mode; Slate Black (`#0B1220`) in dark mode. Flat baseline containing all layout containers.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Containers):** Used for vocabulary drill cards, multiple-choice rows, and input surfaces. In light mode, styled with pure `#FFFFFF` bounded by a crisp 1px solid `neutral-200` (`#E2E8F0`) border. In dark mode, styled with `#111827` bounded by `1px` solid `#263449`.
- **Tier 2 (Interactive Floating / Active):** When an element is pressed or selected (e.g., a chosen quiz option), elevation increases via background fill shifts (`primary-100` / `#E8F2FF`) and border emphasis (`primary-200` / `#BFDBFE`), rather than physical z-axis shadows.
- **Tier 3 (Modals, Sheets & Overlays):** Used for bottom vocabulary sheets and corrective feedback slide-overs. Uses a soft ambient boundary shadow: `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)` in light mode, combined with a `1px` top boundary border.

## Shapes

The shape system balances approachable softness with structure:

- **Cards & Exercise Panels (16px / `rounded-lg`):** Learning modules, question containers, and audio cards use 16px corner radii, imparting warmth and focus without feeling childish.
- **Interactive Controls (12px / `rounded`):** Primary action buttons, multiple choice options, text fields, and audio replay triggers leverage a 12px radius for a clean, handheld feel.
- **Pills & Status Indicators (999px / `rounded-full`):** CEFR level badges (A1–C2), category chips, and audio wave pills use fully circular pill geometries to distinguish metadata from actionable layout cards.
- **Bottom Sheets & Dialogs (24px / `rounded-xl`):** Top corners of bottom-anchored sheets use 24px radius to soften entrance transitions.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action Button:** 
  - Height: `48px` minimum. Full-width on mobile.
  - Shape: `12px` radius.
  - Fill: `primary-600` (`#2169EF`); Text: Pure White, `label-lg`, `600` weight.
  - Hover/Active: `primary-700` (`#1856C7`).
- **Secondary / Container Button:**
  - Fill: `primary-100` (`#E8F2FF`); Text: `primary-700` (`#1856C7`).
  - Active: Fill `primary-200` (`#BFDBFE`).
- **Outlined / Tertiary Button:**
  - Border: `1px` solid `neutral-300` (`#CBD5E1`); Background: transparent; Text: `neutral-800`.
- **Audio Pronunciation Button:**
  - Circular (`48px x 48px`) or pill with `12px` padding.
  - Surface: `primary-100` with `primary-600` speaker icon (`24px`).
  - Active State: Pulsing ring animation with `primary-200` outline.

### 2. Learning & Multiple Choice Cards
- **Baseline Option Card:**
  - Height: Minimum `56px`. Padding: `16px`.
  - Shape: `12px` radius. Background: `neutral-white`. Border: `1.5px` solid `neutral-200`.
  - Text: `body-lg` left-aligned for English options; Arabic/Persian index marker right-aligned.
- **Selected State:**
  - Background: `primary-50` (`#F4F8FF`). Border: `1.5px` solid `primary-600`. Text: `primary-900`.
- **Correct State:**
  - Background: `success-100` (`#DCFCE7`). Border: `1.5px` solid `success-500` (`#22C55E`). Icon: Checkmark in `success-700`.
- **Incorrect State:**
  - Background: `error-100` (`#FEE2E2`). Border: `1.5px` solid `error-500` (`#EF4444`). Icon: Close cross in `error-700`.

### 3. Fill-in-the-Blank & Input Fields
- **Exercise Input:**
  - Minimal inline box with solid `2px` bottom border (`neutral-300`) or boxed `12px` container.
  - Active focus: Border switches to `primary-600` with a subtle `3px` focus ring in `primary-100`.
  - Text typography: `learning-target`, LTR alignment.

### 4. CEFR Level Badges
- **Dimensions:** Pill shape, height `28px`, padding `4px 12px`.
- **Typography:** `label-sm`, weight `700`, uppercase.
- **Colorways by Level:**
  - **A1–A2 (Beginner):** Background `info-100` (`#E0F2FE`), Text `info-700` (`#0369A1`).
  - **B1–B2 (Intermediate):** Background `primary-100` (`#E8F2FF`), Text `primary-700` (`#1856C7`).
  - **C1–C2 (Advanced):** Background `warning-100` (`#FEF3C7`), Text `warning-700` (`#B45309`).

### 5. Progress Indicators
- **Linear Lesson Progress Bar:**
  - Height: `8px`. Track: `neutral-100` (`#F1F5F9`). Corner radius: `999px`.
  - Indicator: Solid `primary-600` (`#2169EF`) with rounded end cap.
- **Mastery Progress Ring:**
  - Stroke width: `6px` or `8px`. Track: `neutral-200`. Indicator: `primary-600` (or `success-500` when unit is complete).
  - Center: Displays numeric percentage or level icon in `title-md`.

### 6. Bottom Navigation Bar
- **Dimensions:** Height `64px`, bottom safe-area offset.
- **Surface:** `neutral-white` in light mode, `neutral-900` in dark mode. Top hairline border `1px` solid `neutral-200`.
- **Items:** 4 to 5 balanced targets. Active item features `primary-600` icon and label (`label-sm`). Inactive items in `neutral-500`.
